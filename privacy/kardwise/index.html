Kardwise — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Kardwise is an offline flashcard app published by kan ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

The short version. Everything you make in Kardwise — decks, cards, study sessions, streaks and settings — stays on your device. We cannot read it and it is never uploaded. The app contains no advertising and no analytics. The only information that ever reaches us is the Google account details you choose to share if you sign in.

What you create stays on your device.

Decks, cards, study sessions, streaks and settings live in a private database inside the app's own storage. Exporting a deck writes a file to a location you choose; importing reads a file you select. We are not involved in either, and no study content is transmitted to us. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

No advertising, and no tracking.

This version of Kardwise displays no ads. No advertising SDK is active, no advertising identifier is read, and the app never asks for tracking permission. If a future version introduces advertising, we will update this policy and the app's store listing before that version is released, and you will be asked for consent wherever the law requires it.

Optional sign-in.

If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive and store your Google account identifier, your email address, your display name and your profile picture URL. We use these solely to identify your account and to attach credits for upcoming features. Signing in is never required — every feature in the app works without it. You can sign out at any time, which removes the session from your device.

What we do not collect.

No analytics, crash-reporting or attribution SDK is included. We do not collect your study content, your answers, your accuracy or your streak. We do not sell personal information.

Children.

Kardwise is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices.

You can use the entire app without an account. If you sign in and want your account data deleted, email kanho.devtech@gmail.com and we will remove it.

Changes.

If this policy changes materially we will update the date above and note the change in the app's release notes.
